Why one hero product

One silhouette, one colourway, a limited size range and a pilot run keep cash and risk small. Many designs multiply samples, stock and sizing risk.

Territories (concepts)

1 Clean court low-top · 2 Minimal everyday lifestyle · 3 Neutral-panel modern. Not approved designs.

Anatomy

Every component has a job.

Materials to compare in sampling (no winner assumed): leather, synthetic leather, microfibre, textile, mesh, suede-look; soles: rubber, EVA, TPR. Compare look, comfort, weight, durability, upkeep, cost, complexity.

Acceptance checks: stitching, bonding, sole attachment, symmetry, colour, size accuracy, eyelet and lace strength, packaging.

Open questions: original design work, small MOQ, custom upper, last, sole, prototypes, revisions, branded packaging. Morgan owns vision, specs, brand, acceptance, marketing and customers. The partner owns feasibility, patterns, sourcing, sampling and production.

Tooling options

A existing sole: cheapest, fastest, least unique. B modified sole: some distinction, depends on supplier. C custom mould: freedom, but cost, ownership and delay risk. Start with A or B unless C is justified. Never exceed the sampling allowance silently.

IP and agreements

In writing: ownership of designs, CAD, patterns and moulds; exclusivity; confidentiality; overrun control; right to move factories; milestones; defect terms. Get legal review. Check trademark Class 25 and possibly 35. Registration is not guaranteed.
